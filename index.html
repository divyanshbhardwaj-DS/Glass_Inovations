About Complete Glass Innovations

A family business built on frameless work.

We're a family-owned glazing business serving Melbourne's inner city and western suburbs. Every job comes back to the same idea: glass that's measured, cut and fitted for the exact space it's going into — not a standard size that's made to fit.

We specialise in all things frameless — splashbacks, showers, balustrades and mirrors — and we keep a same-day service running for cracked or broken glass, because a broken window or shower screen usually can't wait for next week.
