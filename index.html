Knowledge & Standards

Frequently Asked Questions

Pet doors, glass grades, safety compliance, installation timelines and warranties.

How do you size a dog or cat door?

For dogs, measure their back height (shoulder to ground) and chest width — we then CNC cut a flap that clears them easily. Choose Small, Medium or Large in the quote form and we'll confirm exact dimensions on our free measure. Cats use a standard size that suits most breeds, so no measuring is needed.

Can a pet door go into a window or a door?

Yes — our toughened pet doors are fitted through existing glass windows, glass or timber doors, or insulated wall panels. Just tell us where it's going and whether it's upstairs or downstairs so we bring the right gear.

What is the difference between standard clear and Low-Iron glass?

Standard clear glass naturally has a subtle green tint from iron. Low-iron glass (like Starphire) delivers near-true colour clarity — ideal for light-coloured splashbacks, showers, and any panel where you want the view to be perfectly clean.

What Australian Standards must frameless glass comply with?

All structural and architectural glass we fabricate adheres to AS1288:2021 (Glass in Buildings) and AS1926.1 (Swimming Pool Safety). Every panel is thermally toughened safety glass with compliant certification.

How fast can you attend for same-day glass repairs?

For urgent glass emergencies across Melbourne's inner west (Werribee, Footscray, Altona, Newport, Sunshine, etc.), our mobile response vehicle is typically on-site within 1–2 hours to make the perimeter safe and start same-day replacement.
